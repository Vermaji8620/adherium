# Adherium Case Study: Scaling with Lemon.io

## Overview

Adherium, an Australian respiratory remote patient monitoring platform, partnered with Lemon.io to rapidly build engineering capacity for the US market.

## Key Metrics

- **4** developers hired
- **2** days for onboarding
- **1** week to hire first developer

## Company Background

Adherium develops Hailie® SmartInhaler, a connected device paired with an app for patients and healthcare providers managing asthma and COPD. Operating for nearly two decades, the company sought to expand into North American markets with value-based care focus.

## Challenge

David Haddad, Head of Product and Technology, needed to rapidly assemble a team covering backend migrations, frontend development, DevOps, and QA while maintaining lean operations and avoiding communication overhead.

## Solution Approach

### Testing Agency Model First

Haddad initially sought agency partnerships to validate quick value delivery before building in-house teams.

### Cost and Structure Benefits

"It ended up being 50% cheaper," Haddad noted, highlighting how Lemon's direct engineer access eliminated multiple management layers present in traditional agencies.

### Low-Risk Hiring

The ability to engage developers part-time allowed Adherium to "right-size the commitment" while building other products simultaneously.

## Results

### Rapid Team Assembly

Four engineers joined within weeks, delivering immediate impact across infrastructure, architecture, and quality assurance.

### Technical Contributions

- Eugene (platform engineer) identified system improvements within days
- Johnny (senior engineer) restructured the app from device-centric to patient-centered architecture
- Victor and Rodrigo addressed multi-year technical backlog items

### Team Integration

Engineers participated in daily standups, contributed documentation, and collaborated asynchronously across time zones—functioning as permanent team members rather than external contractors.

## Key Takeaway

"They're fantastic engineers who don't sit around twiddling their thumbs. They make stuff happen," Haddad emphasized, describing the partnership as transformational for scaling operations.
